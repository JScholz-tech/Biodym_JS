\chapter{Dynamic Stock Model (DSM)}
\label{ch:dsm}

\section{Concept}

The Dynamic Stock Model tracks material that enters a process and remains in stock for a defined lifetime before leaving as outflow. It uses \emph{cohort-based tracking}: each year's inflow is a separate cohort that decays according to a lifetime distribution.

\begin{equation}
  S(t) = \sum_{\tau=0}^{t} I(\tau) \cdot \left[1 - F_{\text{CDF}}(t - \tau)\right]
\end{equation}

where $S(t)$ is the stock at time $t$, $I(\tau)$ is the inflow in year $\tau$, and $F_{\text{CDF}}$ is the cumulative distribution function of the lifetime.

\section{Categories}

DSM supports multiple categories per process. Each category receives a fraction of the total inflow (\texttt{Inflow\_Split}) and has its own lifetime distribution. This models heterogeneous products --- for example, construction wood split into short-lived (interior) and long-lived (structural) applications.

\begin{notebox}
The inflow splits must sum to 1.0 across all categories.
\end{notebox}

\section{Lifetime Distributions}

Five distribution types are available:

\subsection{Fixed}
All items leave stock at exactly the mean lifetime. Parameters: \texttt{Mean}.

\subsection{Normal}
Symmetric bell curve around the mean. Parameters: \texttt{Mean}, \texttt{StdDev}.

\begin{warnbox}
If \texttt{StdDev} exceeds 80\% of \texttt{Mean}, some lifetimes become negative. Use \emph{FoldedNormal} instead.
\end{warnbox}

\subsection{FoldedNormal}
Like Normal, but negative values are folded to positive. Eliminates the negative lifetime problem. Parameters: \texttt{Mean}, \texttt{StdDev}.

\subsection{LogNormal}
Right-skewed distribution where some items last much longer than average. Always positive. Parameters: \texttt{Mean}, \texttt{StdDev} (of the lognormal curve, not of $\ln$).

\subsection{Weibull}
Flexible distribution controlled by the \texttt{Shape} parameter:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Shape $< 1$: Decreasing failure rate (infant mortality)
  \item Shape $= 1$: Constant failure rate (exponential decay)
  \item Shape $> 1$: Increasing failure rate (wear-out failures)
  \item Shape $\approx 3.6$: Approximates a normal distribution
\end{itemize}
Parameters: \texttt{Mean}, \texttt{Shape}.

See Appendix~\ref{ch:distributions} for the complete mathematical reference.

\section{DSM Outflow Distribution}

Outflows from DSM processes are distributed to downstream flows using the standard TC system. This means DSM outflow splits can be:
\begin{itemize}
  \item Static (constant fractions)
  \item Dynamic (time-varying, from \texttt{2\_3\_dynamic\_TCs})
  \item Subject to Monte Carlo sampling
\end{itemize}

\section{Initial Stock}

DSM processes can start with an existing stock that decays over time. Two methods are available:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Exponential decay}: Simple decay based on mean lifetime
  \item \textbf{ODYM age-cohort}: Rigorous method that reconstructs the age distribution of the initial stock
\end{itemize}

Configure via \texttt{Stock\_Configuration} in the process definition sheet.
