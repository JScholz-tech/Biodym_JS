    1 # Gemini Project Briefing: BioDYM
    2
    3 ## 1. Project Overview
    4
    5 *   **Project Name:** BioDYM MFA Tool
    6 *   **Purpose:** A Python-based tool for performing Material Flow Analysis (MFA) on biological
      systems. It helps track the flow of resources, like biomass, through a defined system to analyze
      efficiency, waste, and environmental impact.
    7 *   **Key Technologies:** Python, Pandas, ODYM framework, Jupyter Notebooks for analysis, Pytest for
      testing.
    8
